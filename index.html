Welcome to LITERACY-BRIDGE

A learning platform designed to help learners develop literacy, numeracy and practical skills through simple lessons, activities and practice.

Start Learning

📖 Learn How to Read

Lesson 1: Letters

Learn the alphabet from A to Z and identify capital and small letters.

  • Recognize letters
  • Say letter sounds
  • Match letters with words

Lesson 2: Sounds

Learn how letters combine to create sounds.

  • Vowel sounds
  • Consonant sounds
  • Simple syllables

Lesson 3: Words

Build simple words from letters and sounds.

  • Two-letter words
  • Three-letter words
  • Common everyday words

Lesson 4: Sentences

Learn how words form meaningful sentences.

  • Capital letters
  • Full stops
  • Reading short sentences

✍️ Learn How to Write

Lesson 1: Handwriting

Practice forming letters clearly and correctly.

Lesson 2: Words

Learn to spell and write common words.

Lesson 3: Sentences

Practice constructing clear and meaningful sentences.

Lesson 4: Paragraphs

Learn how to organize ideas into paragraphs.

📚 Learning Method

1. Learn

Read or watch a simple explanation of the topic.

2. Practice

Complete activities that help you understand the lesson.

3. Apply

Use the new knowledge in real-life situations.

4. Check

Complete exercises to see what you have understood.
