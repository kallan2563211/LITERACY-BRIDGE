Literacy Bridge

A structured learning centre for people building foundational literacy, numeracy and practical life skills. Learn step by step, practise, listen and track progress.

How the Programme Works

1. Learn

Short explanations introduce one skill at a time.

2. Listen

Audio support can read lesson instructions aloud.

3. Practise

Activities let learners use the new skill.

4. Check & Progress

Quizzes and completed lessons show what has been learned.

Future expansion

Teacher accounts, learner profiles, certificates, local-language audio, downloadable lessons, pictures, offline lessons, more quizzes and community learning centres can be added later.
